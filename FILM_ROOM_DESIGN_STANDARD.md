# Merrill Girls Wrestling Film Room

Film Room is a coach-curated learning space, not a social popularity feed.

## Performance-learning design

Every video gives the athlete a job before she watches.

Coach prompt:

**What should athletes watch for?**

Good notes point to one or two observable details:

- Watch her outside foot as she turns the corner.
- Notice the hand position before the shot.
- Watch what she does immediately after giving up the first score.
- Find the moment she creates the angle.

The learning loop is:

**Watch -> Notice -> Discuss -> Try**

This supports the wider Merrill loop:

**Plan -> Do -> Reflect -> Learn -> Adjust -> Repeat**

## Social design

Athletes can:

- mark a video Helpful;
- save it;
- share a team deep link;
- discuss it with teammates and coaches.

Film Room intentionally avoids public popularity totals, follower systems, athlete rankings, and engagement streaks.

Helpful is a private learning signal rather than a public status score.

Comments are team-visible and attached to a coach-curated learning object. There is no private coach-minor messaging.

The comment prompt is:

**What did you notice?**

Athletes are encouraged to describe position, timing, setup, finish, reaction, or another observable detail.

## Video hosting

Merrill stores the URL, coach note, tags, comments, saves, and reactions.

The original platform continues to host the video.

YouTube and Vimeo can play inside Film Room when an embed is available. TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook open on the original platform from the Film Room card.

## Language

Film Room follows `ATHLETE_LANGUAGE_STANDARD.md`.

Use actionable, informational, mastery-oriented language. Point attention toward what the athlete can observe and try.
